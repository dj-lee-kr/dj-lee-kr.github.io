Our research team was selected for the **2025 Promising Young Investigator Teams (PYIT) Research Support Program** at SeoulTech.

## Project information

- **Project:** Efficient Inference and Acceleration System for MoE-Based Large Language Models
- **Program:** 2025 Promising Young Investigator Teams (PYIT) Research Support Program
- **Funding organization:** Seoul National University of Science and Technology
- **Project period:** June 9, 2025 – August 20, 2025

The project investigates expert prefetching, ultra-low-bit model compression, and hardware-friendly acceleration techniques for efficient Mixture-of-Experts inference.
