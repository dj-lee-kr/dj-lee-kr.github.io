I am an M.S. student in the Department of Electrical and Information Engineering at Seoul National University of Science and Technology (SeoulTech). I am a member of the [Intelligent Digital Systems Design Laboratory](https://idsl.seoultech.ac.kr/).

My research interests include **AI accelerators**, **digital system design**, and **deep learning**, with a focus on efficient AI model deployment and hardware-friendly optimization.
