  <section class="project-detail-section">
    <h2>Overview</h2>
    <p>
      This joint research project develops architecture simulation and hardware security technologies for
      high-performance, highly connected chiplet-based SoCs. Led by Ajou University with Seoul National University of
      Science and Technology as a joint research institution, the project covers chiplet-SoC and chiplet-PiM
      architectures, security components, hardware validation, and efficient AI workloads.
    </p>
  </section>

  <section class="project-detail-section">
    <h2>Research focus</h2>
    <ol class="project-detail-focus">
      <li>
        <span>01</span>
        <h3>Architecture simulation</h3>
        <p>Build simulation environments for memory-centric, accelerator-rich, and chiplet-PiM SoC structures.</p>
      </li>
      <li>
        <span>02</span>
        <h3>Hardware security</h3>
        <p>Protect chiplet systems from untrusted memory controllers, MMUs, and third-party chiplets.</p>
      </li>
      <li>
        <span>03</span>
        <h3>Efficient AI processing</h3>
        <p>Reduce LLM data-transfer traffic and support hardware-centric virtualization for multi-tenant workloads.</p>
      </li>
    </ol>
  </section>
